What we charge, and when.

Seeing a price costs nothing. We are paid only when your goods actually ship, as a percentage of the FOB value of the container. No retainer, no subscription, no charge before loading.

Standard fee
5% of FOB value
On orders above $100,000
3% of FOB value
Minimum per shipment
$600
When we invoice
After loading bill of lading date

The rules behind the number

  • One side only. We are paid by you, the buyer, or we are paid by the mill. Never both on the same order. If a mill pays us, that fee is written into the FOB price you see before you commit.
  • Nothing before goods move. Quotes, factory checks, samples and specification work are free. The first invoice is issued after the container loads.
  • The rate is fixed for the order. Once we confirm the fee in writing, it does not change, whatever the mill price does after that.
  • Pass-through costs are at cost. Third-party lab tests, factory audits and pre-shipment inspections are billed at the supplier invoice, with the invoice attached, and only if you ask for them.

If a shipment is cancelled after loading, or the goods fail pre-shipment inspection, no fee is due. We would rather lose the fee than ship a bad container.
